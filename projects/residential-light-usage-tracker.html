Residential Light Usage Tracker

A capstone project designing and building an automated, occupancy- based light shut-off device to reduce household energy costs - from CAD through embedded control.

  • Onshape
  • CAD
  • Arduino
  • Embedded Systems

Overview

This capstone project, started in September 2025, set out to reduce household energy costs by automatically shutting off lights in rooms that aren't occupied. Working with a team of four, I was responsible for the mechanical design and embedded control of a light-switch-mounted shut-off device.

Process

I used Onshape to design the automated shut-off device and modeled five or more CAD parts for the light-switch-mounted mechanism. On the electronics side, I programmed a micro servo motor with an Arduino Nano, iterating through six design revisions before landing on the final version. As a team, we also developed a door sensor and companion app to collect light usage data across the house.

Results

The finished device monitors occupancy counts inside a room and automatically turns off residential lights once the room is empty - cutting unnecessary energy use without requiring any change in behavior from the people living there.
