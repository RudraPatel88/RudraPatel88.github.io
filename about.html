About me

Background

I grew up in Marlton, NJ, where I spent years on competitive robotics teams before coming to Penn. That's where most of my hands-on engineering habits started - writing motor controllers, managing a GitHub repo for a whole team, and learning that a design isn't done until it's been tested and broken a few times. At Penn, I'm involved with ASME, Penn Aerial Robotics (PennAiR), Penn Jet Propulsion, and the Wharton Undergraduate Aerospace Club (WUAC).

Outside of engineering

I mentored students at Unified Robotics and the Aeronautics Club, teaching build cycles, flight principles, and problem-solving to younger and newer members. I also volunteered as a teacher with the India Temple Association and worked as a cybersecurity intern with the New Jersey Cybersecurity and Communications Integration Cell.
