About me

I'm a mechanical engineering student at the University of Pennsylvania, working toward a BSE in Mechanical Engineering and Applied Mechanics (expected May 2030). I like problems that move between CAD and hardware - control systems, embedded electronics, and building things that actually have to work at a competition or in someone's house.

Background

I grew up in Marlton, NJ, where I spent years on competitive robotics teams before coming to Penn. That's where most of my hands-on engineering habits started - writing motor controllers, managing a GitHub repo for a whole team, and learning that a design isn't done until it's been tested and broken a few times. At Penn, I'm involved with ASME, Penn Aerial Robotics (PennAiR), and Penn Jet Propulsion.

Outside the lab

I mentored students at Unified Robotics and the Aeronautics Club, teaching build cycles, flight principles, and problem-solving to younger and newer members. I also volunteered as a teacher with the India Temple Association and worked as a cybersecurity intern with the New Jersey Cybersecurity and Communications Integration Cell. Outside of engineering, I speak English, Gujarati, and Italian, and I like biking, skiing, and staying active in my free time.
